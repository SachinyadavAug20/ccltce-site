How it works

1

Pick a quest

Choose any weekly prompt above — or invent your own. Beginner tracks welcome.

2

Build & push

Create a repo, commit as you go, write a short README. Ugly-but-shipped beats perfect-but-private.

3

Share the repo

Drop your repo link in #showcase on the Community page for XP and feedback.

4

Explain it

Present at the next meetup — what broke, what you learned. Shipping it is half; explaining it is the XP.
